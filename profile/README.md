# Klectr

**Independent software studio. Software that ships.**

Klectr is the professional home of Triston Armstrong — a contract engineer who embeds with teams and ships his own tools on the side. Everything here is software built to solve real problems, not demos or portfolio filler.

## Products

- **[Zimer](https://zimer.klectr.dev)** — Time tracking for invoicing. Timer to invoice in two clicks; self-host free forever, or use the hosted version.
- **[Zlern](https://zlern.com)** — Homeschool planning for families. Lessons, scheduling, and progress tracking in one place.
- **[Zlorb](https://github.com/ArmstrongOSS/zlorb)** — A lightweight Rust CI/CD daemon. Watches your git repos, fast-forward pulls, and builds on change — hands-free deploys.

## Contract Work

Triston works as an augment-type 1099 contract developer — he joins your team, learns your stack, and ships. Not a consulting shop, not an agency. One engineer, embedded.
